Aura and the Empty Tunnels

The Black Ant colony was a whirlwind of disciplined activity beneath the great oak tree. Every ant scurried with purpose, carrying crumbs and seeds. Among them was Aura, known throughout the tunnels not for her size, but for her unmatched speed and cleverness. Although she was often tasked with simple scouting duties, Aura dreamed of protecting her kingdom from its most ancient enemy: the Red Ants. For years, the two colonies had lived in a tense, silent rivalry. However, today was different. A frantic messenger burst into the main chamber, gasping out the news: “The Red Ants have begun construction! They are using small stones and sap to build a wall near the Clover Field—the best foraging ground!” Queen Vita struck her antennae against the stone floor. “This is an act of war!” Aura, meanwhile, listened carefully. She knew losing that field meant certain starvation for many. Therefore, she immediately stepped forward. “My Queen,” Aura said, “Allow me to go. I will scout the construction and find a weakness. ” The Queen commanded, “Go, Aura. But be careful. We need information, not a battle.”

Aura moved with silent precision. First, she had to cross the wide, open dirt path without being seen. After a difficult journey, she reached the Red Ant construction site. Peering under a dry leaf, she spotted King Rix planning with his advisor, General Varr. The conversation confirmed her fears: "The wall is a good start, but it isn't enough... However, if we launch a direct attack on their main food storage tonight, we can end this quickly... Therefore, While they are distracted by the wall, the main force strikes their storage tunnels at midnight." Aura broke cover and began her frantic run back. Just as she reached the open path, the sky suddenly broke open, turning into a torrential downpour. The dirt path quickly transformed into a thick, muddy trap. Aura tried to run, but her tiny legs sank with every step. Although the main path was impossible, she spotted a cluster of fungus. Finally, she climbed onto the fungus. It was a slippery, treacherous shortcut, yet it kept her above the worst of the mud.


Aura stumbled into the main tunnel just moments before the midnight watch began. Immediately, she was rushed to Queen Vita. "My Queen! The wall is a trick! In fact, the main attack is tonight—on the food storage!" Queen Vita’s face hardened. "Although we are prepared to fight, we are also smart," she announced. "Therefore, we will not fight their midnight attack; we will empty the tunnels!" Orders flew out, directing every Black Ant to quickly move the winter supply to a secret, empty chamber. At midnight, King Rix and his shock troops burst into the storage tunnels, expecting mountains of seeds. Instead, they found nothing but dust and a single, carefully placed sign: The Black Ant Kingdom invites you to negotiate. King Rix was furious yet defeated. Consequently, he had no choice. The next morning, a tired, mud-caked Aura watched as King Rix of the Red Ants was forced to crawl to Queen Vita's chamber, not for war, but for peace. From that day on, Aura was not just known for her speed, but for the wisdom that saved the entire kingdom. The Black Ants realized that true strength lay not in the size of their soldiers, but in the brilliance of their scouts.
